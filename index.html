Music restoration audio examples

Compare the same 15-second passage across a clean reference, the original degraded input, its codec reconstruction, and three restoration methods. The codec reconstruction shows the effect of the codec round trip. Playback levels are matched by scalar gain, except for the volume example, which preserves relative levels. Recordings were selected for clean quality, and examples were fixed using input-only criteria before comparing restoration outputs. Some recordings appear under multiple degradations. These illustrative examples are separate from the blinded listening study.
