Music restoration audio examples

Open any example and compare the same 15-second passage across a clean reference, the original degraded input, its codec reconstruction, and three restoration methods. The codec-reconstructed input isolates the codec round trip. Playback levels are matched within each example by scalar gain. Examples were selected for clean recording quality and audible input degradation before comparing restoration outputs. These illustrative examples are separate from the blinded listening study.
